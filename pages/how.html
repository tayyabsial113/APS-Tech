FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you want to know before working with us.

How long does a typical project take? +
It depends on the scope. A focused RPA automation can be live in 2–4 weeks. A full custom software build typically takes 3–6 months. We give you an accurate timeline after the discovery phase — never a guess.
Do you work with small businesses or only enterprises? +
Both. While most of our clients are mid-market to enterprise, we've also worked with ambitious startups that want to scale intelligently from day one. Our engagement model flexes to fit your budget and team size.
What happens after the project is delivered? +
Every deployment comes with a post-launch support period. Beyond that, we offer ongoing managed services, SLA-backed support contracts, and continuous improvement retainers — so your solution stays healthy and evolves with your business.
How do you handle data security and confidentiality? +
Security is non-negotiable for us. We sign NDAs before discovery, work within ISO 27001 frameworks, and can operate within your own infrastructure if required. All team members are vetted and trained in data handling protocols.
Can you integrate with our existing systems? +
Yes — this is one of our core strengths. We've integrated with SAP, Salesforce, Oracle, custom ERPs, legacy COBOL systems, and everything in between. If it has an API (or even if it doesn't), we can connect it.
What does the free consultation include? +
A 45-minute call with one of our solution architects. We'll listen to your challenges, assess your automation potential, and give you an honest recommendation — whether that's working with us or not. No sales pressure, just straight talk.
