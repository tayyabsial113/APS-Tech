Our Story

We Started with a Simple Mission

APS Tech was founded with one conviction: that businesses should spend their energy on what matters — innovation, growth, and people — not on repetitive, manual processes that machines can handle better.

What started as a small RPA consultancy in Lahore has grown into a full-service technology partner serving enterprises across healthcare, finance, logistics, and beyond — across 4 continents.

Today, our team of 80+ engineers, automation architects, AI specialists, and designers work together to build solutions that create real, measurable business impact for every client we serve.
